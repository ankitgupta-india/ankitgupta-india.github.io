Meet the Statistician

Er. Ankit Gupta (B.E. & P.G.D.M.), Founder & Principal Consultant
Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India

“My mission is simple: to empower Indian academics and PhD scholars with robust, transparent, and reproducible statistical analysis—so you can focus on the insights, not the code.”

With over 13 years of diverse corporate experience under different roles. And with over additional 7 years of private consulting practice experience working with faculty and doctoral candidates, Er. Gupta offers a fusion of academic rigour and industry-grade Analysis. His credentials include:

  • Serving as lead statistical architect for PhD theses and related publications. These span across medicine, education, life sciences, and public health among others.
  • Designing proprietary R automation libraries for assumption diagnostics, power/sample-size computation, and publication-ready visualizations.

My Mission & Vision as a PhD statistical consultant in India

At PhDStats Advisor, my mission is to empower Indian professors, PhD scholars, and professional researchers with high-quality, ethical, and reproducible statistical consulting. I believe rigorous data analysis should be transparent, turnkey, and actionable—letting you focus on discovery while I handle methodology, coding, and interpretation. By using domain-agnostic statistical expertise I aim to become the go-to partner for academic and industry research projects across medicine, education, life sciences, public health, and beyond .
