I believe in the prowess of an Indian mind; Individuality; power of fact led, objective oriented innovation; structure and clarity. With the help of these tools I can easily navigate chaos and complexity.

Way I Work

Aggressive Attitude – Sometimes a problem requires an attack from all sides

Original Approach – Taking an original path requires risk taking and having foresight

Analytical Depth – Involves asking a lot of questions and recursive introspection

Strategic Thinking – Is like imagining where you want to be tomorrow, today; and answering “How did I get here?”

Grand Designs – Seldom do goals worth achieving happen by chance

Key frameworks I use

Agile Theory – Systematic, modular, incremental and automated workflows can counter both chaos and complexity

Game Theory – Each unique result of a game requires its own unique path

Chaos Theory – Simplyfing chaos through interconnected sub-systems

Blue Oceans Theory – Making the obvious irrelevant through the uncontested

Data Synthesis – Making luck with data and analysis

My natural abilities

Natural abilities towards strategy development, execution planing, data modeling and automated data analysis. I thrive under chaotic and complex conditions.

How I look at businesses

I do not look at businesses as individual entities, I look at them as ecosystems. Not in the way porter did (The five forces), but somewhat an extension of it. In this the industry is looked at as a system of interconnected sub-systems. By looking at it this way the ecosystem reduces to just three things: 1) Players 2) Opportunities and 3) Products.

Clients I have worked for as a corporate intelligence consultant

Corporate Planning Departments of Thermax (Pune HO), Henkel (Mumbai HO), Mitsui (Japan HO), ista (Germany HO), GE (Bangalore & Gurgaon), Siemens (Mumbai & Vadodara), Asian Paints (Mumbai HO), Pidilite (Mumbai HO), Godrej (Mumbai HO), Hindware (Gurgaon HO), among others

Domain knowledge

Worked with clients in more than 20 Industries & Sectors across all stakeholder categories spanning through different economic levels. Engaged with all hierarchical client team members and decision leaders. Executed projects with regional, organizational, divisional and pan India focus. Projects executed across more than 50 locations in India including both onsite and offsite. This domain knowledge acquired over more than 15 years of deep observation and experimentation in a highly competitive and fast paced environment. Furthermore, information from secondary sources was complimented with intense primary research.
